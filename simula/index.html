Interactive AI simulator for managing the information space in crisis situations

Mobile app: role-based news scenarios, simulated reactions and feedback on messages.

Open in Google Play

An interactive information field simulator for press office specialists to practice crisis situations. It improves teamwork by simulating media reactions to different events, involving all employees in exercises, coordinating communication, and rehearsing responses until they become instinctive.

Training press office specialists

To communicate with mass audiences in exceptional situations.

Modeling mass audience behavior

Across different communication channels.

Analyzing employee interaction

And improving the effectiveness of their communication.

Partner and key customer

“The simulator is a unique solution for training PR departments at large companies and government bodies to act in a crisis. Any information crisis brings a flood of requests from media and citizens, dozens and hundreds of different media publications, and an aggressive social media response. Recreating this in a training exercise is extremely difficult. Our software can simulate a complex situation, whether a banking crisis or an industrial accident, and assess PR departments’ readiness.”
Andrey Timonov
Director of Communications, Rosenergoatom
