Interactive AI simulator for managing the information space in crisis situations

A unique digital trainer for PR teams. The system improves every aspect of team interaction by simulating media reactions to news events, involving all employees in exercises, improving communication, and sharpening responses until they become instinctive.

01.

How it works

Users take part in exercises with artificial intelligence that simulates the information space around specific events (non-routine, emergency, or news events).

02.

Who it is for

The system is intended for PR teams and specialists whose job is to communicate in the information space.

03.

Workflow

The facilitator creates an exercise, invites participants, and assigns roles. Participants imitate people in the information space by creating messages.

TRAINING PROCESS

  1. Preparation

    The facilitator starts a new exercise, invites participants, and assigns roles:

    • “Press Center” — the company’s official representative
    • “Media” — news agency employees
    • “Social Networks” — ordinary users
    • “Moderator” — the facilitator’s assistant

  2. Start

    Once roles are assigned, the exercise starts at a set time and participants join.

  3. Case

    The facilitator announces a fictional non-routine, emergency, or news event that has supposedly just happened.

  4. Exercise

    Participants process the event and prepare information according to their roles:

    • “Press Center” creates official company statements that become available to others after publication
    • “Media” creates news stories based on the official statements
    • “Social Networks” comments on and interacts with the news, according to the role’s behavior patterns

  5. Results

    Moderators monitor participants’ work and adjust their actions.

  6. Final

    After the exercise, moderators export the training history, assess participants’ actions, and prepare recommendations.
