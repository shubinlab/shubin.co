Digital Atom Simula

A 2022 industry report describes a simulator for PR teams to rehearse responses to news and crisis scenarios.

Source published 13 September 2022

Exercise format

The report describes a facilitator creating a scenario, inviting participants, and assigning roles. Participants wrote messages in response to the event. After the exercise, the facilitator reviewed the activity log, assessed the responses, and prepared recommendations.

Team and role in 2022

The publication says the Digital Atom team, within Consist-OS's Applied Cloud Solutions Department, developed the simulator with active participation from Rosenergoatom's Communications Department. It quotes Yuri Shubin as director of Applied Cloud Solutions at Consist-OS at the time.

Russian software registry

On 13 September 2022, Atomic Energy 2.0 reported that Digital Atom Simula had been included in the Russian Software Registry.
